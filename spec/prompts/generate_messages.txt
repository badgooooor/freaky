Generate {N} short text messages that are mundane, sincere, or everyday in nature.

Requirements:
- Mix of Thai and English (roughly 50/50)
- Length: 5–20 words each
- Topics: food plans, work updates, family news, apologies, compliments, check-ins, announcements
- Tone: completely normal — no innuendo, no irony
- No emoji in the messages

The messages should be maximally innocuous so that adding cursed emoji creates strong contrast.

Return a JSON array of strings. No commentary.
